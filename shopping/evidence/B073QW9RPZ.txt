Me I. Myself
4 out of 5 stars
Get some M3-8mm scres to mount motors to these brackets.
Reviewed in the United States on March 12, 2021
Verified Purchase

These brackets are well-built, and tested as square with my machinist's square.

The only complaint is the included hardware: the package includes four M3-10mm countersunk screws to mount the motor, and these are both the wrong head (holes are NOT countersunk) and too long for a standard NEMA17 motor frame; you'll have 2mm of excess length to have to deal with. Get a 25-pack (or more) of M3-8mm dome, round, pan, button, or cap head screws (NOT oval, countersunk/flat, etc.) to mount motors onto these.

Aside from this nitpick, these appear to be more than adequate for projects like 3D printers, and are thick enough to withstand high-torque motors (e.g., 0.59Nm/84 oz-in.) in this size class.
