Structural Design - A Mecanum wheel consists of a central hub surrounded by multiple freely rotating rollers mounted at a 45° angle relative to the wheel’s plane. These rollers, made of polyurethane or rubber, are arranged diagonally along the wheel’s circumference. The angled rollers act as passive wheels, enabling lateral motion when driven. The number of rollers are 9 per wheel, with their orientation alternating between left and right angles can effectively balances forces.
Omnidirectional Mobility - Unlike conventional wheels, Mecanum wheels allow holonomic motion—movement in any direction without changing the wheel’s orientation—by combining rotational and lateral forces. This unique capability has made them indispensable in warehouse(automated guided vehicles-AGVs), industrial robots(Multi angle collaboration on assembly lines), stage equipment smooth horizontal movement, special vehicles(Firefighting robots for narrow space rescue).
Robotic AI Learning - This robot mecanum wheel kit is suitable for the STEAM education, which can work with robot chassis kits and controllers like Arduino uno R3, Raspberry Pi and more, suit for most robotic educational science requirements. Based on this mecanum wheel, by the help of e.g., Arduino, Raspberry, Microbit and other MCUs, then you can learn how to code programming to realize the robot function, e.g., Move forward, backword, left turn, right turn, and other functions.
Suitable for DIY - Widely compat with robot kit, an integration solution for robotics learning, custom DIY projects, especially for students, makers, hobbyists and teachers.
