Nothin' fancy, but it does the job.
Reviewed in the United States on February 23, 2026
Color: Black-M5
Verified Purchase

These aren't "premium" threaded fasteners by any standard, but they work well enough for non-precision applications.

I use these for general structural fittings and anywhere I don't need an accuracy of "1/10th of a gnat's ass".

for precision applications requiring specific torques, repeatble positional accuracy, etc., I default to the smoother threads of higher-grade stainless Steel fasteners.

I knocked off a star because there was an oily film on most of the pieces (residue from the manufacturing process, I assume?) that I had to clean off before use.
