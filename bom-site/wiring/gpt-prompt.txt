=== DIAGRAM 1-power: Hound 1 — power distribution (pack side and logic island) ===

CONNECTORS (pin order is the physical order on the part):
  PACK — HOOVO 3S 2200 mAh 80C LiPo — T-plug (Deans)
     pins: V+ | GND
     NOTES: 11.1 V nominal, 12.6 V full, 10.5 V floor (cutoff V 10.5 in firmware). A pack that reads 6.8 V is dead: never charge it.
  ESTOP — E-STOP — NC mushroom switch in the + lead
     pins: IN | OUT
     NOTES: Latching, twist-release, mounted at deck X −140 (assembly.py). Breaks EVERYTHING on the pack side; the Pi keeps running on the bank.
  WAGO_P — Wago 221-415 5-way — PACK + (11.1 V)
     pins: pigtail + | shield VMOT | XL4015 IN+ | LM2596 IN+ | 47k divider
     NOTES: Lever nut, 24–12 AWG. Slot 1 takes the 14 AWG pigtail, the rest 22 AWG. This and its twin are the ONLY pack-side splices.
  WAGO_N — Wago 221-415 5-way — PACK − = STAR GROUND
     pins: pigtail − | shield GND | XL4015 IN− | LM2596 IN− | 10k divider
     NOTES: Every return comes back here on its own wire. Never through a board GND pin, never through a USB cable.
  SHIELD_PWR — CNC Shield v3 — power screw terminal
     pins: VMOT | GND
     NOTES: 1000 µF 25 V electrolytic across VMOT/GND at the terminal, stripe (−) to GND. Reversed = vents on first power-up.
  XL4015 — XL4015 5 A buck — trim OUT to 6.0 V
     pins: IN+ | IN− | OUT+ | OUT−
     NOTES: Screw terminals. Set 6.00 V on the meter with nothing on OUT, then wire the PCA9685. Heatsink on. 4 A continuous derated.
  LM2596 — LM2596 3 A buck — trim OUT to 5.0 V
     pins: IN+ | IN− | OUT+ | OUT−
     NOTES: Screw terminals. Set 5.00 V on the meter with nothing on OUT. 2 A continuous without a fan. Vin must stay ≥ 6.5 V (pack floor 10.5 V: fine).
  PCA_PWR — PCA9685 servo-power screw terminal

  FAN — 40 mm 5 V fan on the driver heatsinks

  WAGO5_P — Wago 221-415 5-way — 5 V rail +

  WAGO5_N — Wago 221-415 5-way — 5 V rail −

     pins: A3 | GND
     NOTES: The 4-pin block Abort/Hold/Resume/CoolEn on the shield's edge; CoolEn is A3. Its GND is the neighbouring GND pin.
  BANK — USB power bank 5 V / 3 A (logic island)
     pins: USB-A out
     NOTES: Genuine 3 A bank. Cable ≤ 0.3 m, ≥ 24 AWG power conductors. Powers ONLY the Pi; the Pi powers the R4, the hub, the adapters and the webcam.
  PI_PWR — Raspberry Pi 4 — USB-C power in

     pins: D2 X STEP | D5 X DIR | D3 Y STEP | D6 Y DIR | D4 Z STEP | D7 Z DIR | D12 A STEP | D13 A DIR | D8 EN (all four) | A3 VBAT sense | A4 SDA (option B) | A5 SCL (option B) | USB-C → Pi hub
     NOTES: Stack the shield on the R4; the pins listed are what the shield takes. Firmware r4_drive v1.0-best-drive: 30 kHz step ISR, 1/32, 415 mm/s. Logic 5 V. USB from the Pi hub; ENABLE_WIFI 0.

=== PROCEDURES AND WARNINGS (print these on the sheet; they come from the electrical model wiring.py) ===
  [WARN] webcam sees 4.42 V at its 500 mA descriptor (4.58 V at a typical 250 mA) through its own captive 1.5 m cable; USB floor 4.75 V, most cameras run to ~4.4 V. If it drops out at 1080p, run 720p — its cable cannot be shortened
  [WARN] A 1 m 28 AWG phone cable to the Pi would drop 0.95 V → 3.95 V at the Pi = brownout. The bank cable is a spec: ≤ 0.3 m with ≥ 24 AWG power conductors (any '3 A fast-charge' cable)
  [WARN] Motor phases run 1.5 A through Dupont 2.54 contacts rated 2.0 A — inside rating but the crimp quality is the margin. A contact that heats in the wheels-up test gets re-crimped or soldered.
  [WARN] Tilt servo stall 2.5 A through the PCA9685's 2.54 mm channel header (rated 2.0 A). Momentary, and how every servo board works; keep stalls short with tilt limits in software
  [WARN] DRV8825 nENABLE has an internal pull-down: during the R4's ~1 s reset window (boot, watchdog, sketch upload) D8 floats and all four drivers are ENABLED at full holding current. Add a 10 kΩ pull-up from the shield's EN pin to its 5 V header (consumables). The R4-unpowered case is still the power-up order
  [WARN] No fuse on the pack and no cell alarm (declined). A 2200 mAh 80C pack delivers >70 A into a short; the T-plug is a disconnect, not protection. Listed under Safety as reviewer-recommended.
  [WARN] Pi 5 only: default 600 mA USB cap → set usb_max_current_enable=1, or use the Pi 4 this file assumes.
  [VERIFY] Load-test the bank at 2.5 A for 60 s with the meter on the Pi's 5 V pin: below 4.8 V the bank is not a 3 A bank.
  [VERIFY] If the bank's output is USB-C and the Pi 4 is an early board (pre-2020, rev 1.1), an e-marked C-to-C cable will not power it — use an A-to-C cable or a plain C-to-C. Later Pi 4s and all Pi 5s are fine
  [VERIFY] Before connecting SDA/SCL: power each I2C module alone and meter SDA to GND — it must read ≤ 3.3 V. A module whose pull-ups go to 5 V goes on 3.3 V power or not on the Pi.
  [VERIFY] BCM 4/5 have NO on-board I2C pull-ups (BCM 2/3 have 1.8 kΩ). The MLX90640 module must carry its own, or add 4.7 kΩ from SDA and SCL to 3.3 V (consumables). And w1-gpio (1-Wire) defaults to BCM 4 — leave that overlay off
  [VERIFY] PCA9685 VCC is 3.3 V, so the tilt servo gets 3.3 V PWM. MG996R clones normally accept it; if the servo jitters or ignores commands, that is the level. Do NOT raise VCC to 5 V — the board's I2C pull-ups follow VCC and would put 5 V on the Pi's SDA/SCL. A 74HCT125 buffer fixes it
  [PROCEDURE] 1000 µF electrolytic across VMOT: stripe = negative, to the GND terminal. Reversed, it vents on the first power-up
  [PROCEDURE] CSI v2: ESP32 #2 runs csi_tx OFF the rover (dropped as far along the wall as the room allows, any USB 5 V, channel 6, 100 Hz probes). ESP32 #3 runs csi_rx_udp ON the rover from the LM2596 5 V pin — USB never connected while the 5 V pin is fed — and streams the CSI of every probe to the Pi hotspot, which must be on channel 6, UDP port 5005. ESP32 #1 is the spare.
  [PROCEDURE] DRV8825 into the shield socket with its EN pin over the socket's EN label (the trimpot ends up toward the shield's power terminal on genuine Pololu layouts; clones vary — go by the label). Backwards = dead driver on power-up, no warning
  [PROCEDURE] The 40 mm fan must be a 5 V fan for the LM2596 rail. A 12 V fan goes on the pack side (Wago +/−, 11.1 V nominal) instead — check the label before it is wired
  [PROCEDURE] Servo return: PCA9685 V+ GND terminal → XL4015 OUT− (its own wire). Motor return: shield GND terminal → Wago. Neither ever through a board GND pin or a USB cable.
  [PROCEDURE] LM2596 OUT feeds five loads (fan, two LD2410C, LD2450, ESP32 #3): six conductors per polarity. Fan straight off the LM2596 OUT terminals, the four sensors from a 5-way Wago pair. Never daisy-chain through a load's connector
  [PROCEDURE] AS BUILT (v1.0 best drive): DRV8825 1/32 = M0 + M2 → caps on MS1 and MS3 under each driver, MS2 open (eight caps), plus the D12 and D13 jumpers on the A header = ten jumpers total. Caps go on before the drivers. 1/16 (one cap, MS3 only, firmware m 16) is the faster alternative — a drive-setting change, ask first.
  [PROCEDURE] Motor coil pairs by multimeter: the two leads that show ~2 Ω are one coil. A coil split across the 2B/2A–1A/1B header halves makes a motor buzz and not turn.
  [PROCEDURE] Each radar's GND gets TWO wires: one to the LM2596 OUT− Wago (its power return, 260 mA for the three) and one to its CP2102's GND pin (signal reference). Never power a radar from the CP2102's 5 V pin: that is the Pi USB budget, and the return would run through the Pi
  [PROCEDURE] Power-up: bank → Pi → R4 (USB) FIRST, then the pack at the T-plug. DRV8825 nENABLE floats LOW = enabled; with VMOT alone four motors sit at full holding current with nothing commanding them.
  [PROCEDURE] Pack-voltage sense: 47 kΩ from the Wago + to the shield CoolEn pin (A3), 10 kΩ from A3 to the shield GND pin. 12.6 V → 2.21 V at A3 (R4 ADC ref 5 V). Then enable the cutoff with V 10.5 over serial; until the divider exists vcut stays 0.
  [PROCEDURE] Read the sense resistor on one driver: R100 → Vref = 0.75 V for 1.5 A; R200 → 1.50 V. Set all four with the multimeter, motors unplugged, before any motor is connected.

=== DECISION ALREADY MADE: which bus drives the PCA9685 ===
AS BUILT = diagram 3A: the Raspberry Pi drives the PCA9685 over I2C-1 (BCM 2/3), 3.3 V logic, VCC = Pi 3V3. The software (turret.py, station.py) is written for this. Diagram 3B (PCA9685 on the CNC Shield's A4/A5, 5 V logic, VCC = shield 5V, driven by the UNO R4) is the ALTERNATIVE, only if the servo misbehaves on 3.3 V PWM; it requires new R4 firmware. Draw both, mark 3A "AS BUILT" and 3B "ALTERNATIVE — NOT WIRED". Never wire both at once: the PCA9685 must never see 5 V pull-ups while its SDA/SCL touch the Pi.

=== SELF-CHECK (run it, print PASS/FAIL per line) ===
1. Every CONNECTOR:pin used in CONNECTIONS exists in that connector's pin list.
2. Each LD2410C and the LD2450 has exactly TWO GND wires: one to WAGO5_N, one to its CP2102 GND. Their VCC/5V comes from WAGO5_P, never from the CP2102's 5V pin.
3. Nothing on the Pi 40-pin header carries 5 V: all I2C/I2S devices are on 3V3 pins; PCA9685 VCC = 3V3 in 3A.
4. The MLX90640 is on BCM 4/5 (i2c3), alone. The two VL53L1X and the PCA9685 are on BCM 2/3 (I2C-1). Addresses on I2C-1: 0x40, 0x29, 0x30 — no clash.
5. Motor bus: WAGO_P → SHIELD_PWR VMOT and WAGO_N → SHIELD_PWR GND, 22 AWG, with the 1000 µF cap noted, stripe to GND.
6. XL4015 OUT− goes to the PCA9685 GND TERMINAL by its own wire (servo return); it is not shared with any signal ground.
7. The E-STOP sits in the pack + lead between the T-plug and WAGO_P; the Pi is on the USB bank, not on the pack.
8. The divider is 47 kΩ WAGO_P → A3, 10 kΩ A3 → WAGO_N; A3 is the shield's CoolEn pin.
9. ESP32-S3 no.3 is fed at its 5V pin from WAGO5_P and its USB port is marked "never while 5V is fed".
10. The shield's four motor headers map X=BL, Y=FL, Z=BR, A=FR; each motor's coil pairs are found with a meter, colours are indicative only.
11. Power-up order is printed: bank → Pi → R4 (USB) → then the pack.
