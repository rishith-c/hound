VERDICT
  Four motors carry the rover at design mass 5.11 kg with the lowest passing
  setting: DRV8825, no cooling. Governing case is C (5° ramp): the smallest margin
  in every row. At the 6 kg ceiling, case C fails at every current — keep mass
  under ~5.2 kg or accept level floors only.

  What fails first: a 5° ramp on carpet at 0.3 m/s². The motors stall silently —
  steppers do not slow down, they stop and lose position with no error.
  Next: measure pull-out torque at 24 rpm, 1.5 A, on the actual battery with a
  lever arm and the hanging scale. That one number retires K_DYN.

NOT ANALYSED: fatigue, buckling, impact, vibration, roller wear, uneven floors,
  wheel unloading, combined-slip mecanum behaviour, thermal equilibrium of motors
  or drivers, battery sag under stall, firmware timing. None of this is a
  substitute for the torque measurement above.
